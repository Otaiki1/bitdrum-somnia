# BitDrum Motion Graphics Walkthrough Script

## Overview

- Format: Motion graphics product walkthrough
- Recommended runtime: 75 to 90 seconds
- Goal: Explain what BitDrum is, show how the app works, and leave viewers with a clear sense of speed, intelligence, and competitive energy
- Visual source: Frontend elements from the BitDrum landing page, Arena, The Core, Leaderboard, and Portfolio views

## Voice-Over Direction

- Tone: Confident, sharp, modern, and slightly cinematic
- Pacing: Fast enough to feel energetic, but never rushed
- Delivery: Clean and precise, with emphasis on words like "call", "signal", "live", "settle", and "edge"
- Voice reference: A polished tech-brand narrator with trader energy, not hype-man energy

## Sound Design Direction

- Use soft UI blips for stat reveals and tab changes
- Use low sub hits for major screen transitions
- Use rising synth sweeps for signal reveals and trade confirmation moments
- Use short digital ticks for countdowns and live market updates
- Use a satisfying metallic shimmer for leaderboard and payout moments

## Background Music Suggestion

- Style: Dark, futuristic electronic with a steady pulse
- Tempo: Around 118 to 124 BPM
- Feel: Clean techno pulse mixed with cinematic fintech energy
- Avoid: Overly aggressive EDM, distracting vocals, or playful sounds
- Reference mood: A premium launch-video bed with tension, momentum, and space for narration

## Editor Notes

- Keep transitions sleek and directional, with frequent push-ins and layered parallax
- Gold should signal action, reward, and status
- Cyan and blue should signal intelligence, telemetry, and live data
- Use quick zooms and masked reveals around The Core to make it feel like a living system
- Let the Arena section move fastest, since it is the heart of the walkthrough
- Let the final montage breathe for the last two seconds so the brand lands clearly
